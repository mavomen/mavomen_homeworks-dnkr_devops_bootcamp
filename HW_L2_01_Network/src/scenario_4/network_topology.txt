# Network Topology

## 1. Current (assumed) Company Topology

- 1 Router (Gateway: 192.168.1.1)
- 1 Switch
- 3 Servers: Web (192.168.1.10), DB (192.168.1.11), Cache (192.168.1.12)
- 10 User computers (192.168.1.100 - 192.168.1.110)

All devices connect to the central switch (Star topology); the switch connects
to the router, which provides internet access.

## 3. What Happens If the Switch Fails?

In a Star topology the switch is the single point of failure. If it dies:

- Every device connected to it (all 3 servers + all 10 users) loses
  connectivity instantly, both between each other and to the router/internet.
- The router itself is still alive, and the servers/users are still running,
  but no traffic can pass through the failed switch.
- Recovery: replace/reboot the switch; until then the whole LAN is down.

Note: this is why production setups use redundant switches (two stacked/core
switches) to avoid a single point of failure.
